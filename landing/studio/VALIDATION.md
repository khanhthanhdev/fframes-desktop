# First-time visitor comprehension test

Status: **Pending — 0 of 5 participants tested.** No unfamiliar participants or recruiting channel were available in this session. Automated browser checks and simulated personas do not count as people. Do not report a success rate until these sessions are run.

Recruit five people who have never used or seen fframes Studio. Prefer people who do not write code or edit video professionally. Test individually, on the device they normally use. Record device, date and their exact words, with their consent. Do not introduce the product before showing the page.

## Moderator script

1. Say: “Please look at this page as you normally would when discovering a new product. You have 15 seconds, and you can scroll. Please don't click anything yet.”
2. Hide the page. Ask, without hints: “In your own words, what does this product do?”
3. Ask: “Suppose you already made a video and wanted to change the title in one scene. How would you do that?”
4. Restore the page. Ask: “You want to try this product. What would you click first?” Record their first indicated or actual click. Stop before downloading or installing anything.
5. Only after recording all three answers, ask: “What was unclear?” and “What would you expect to need before making your first video?” Do not use these follow-ups to repair or rescore the original answers.

## Scoring rubric, fixed before testing

| Question            | Correct without help                                                                                                                                                        | Incorrect or incomplete                                                                                                 |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What does it do?    | Describes making an animated video from a written description with an AI assistant. Everyday equivalents count; no need to mention code.                                    | Only says “video editor”; assumes a text overlay tool, stock-footage search, or generated live-action footage.          |
| How would you edit? | Select the relevant scene in the timeline (or a supported object/time range), then type a request to change the title in the assistant/request box and send it.             | Only says “ask AI” without choosing the target; assumes universal direct text editing or dragging replaces the wording. |
| What first click?   | Points to or clicks “Get Studio” or the Windows download button in the install section. A non-Windows participant may explain that it requires Windows and identify that button as the intended starting action. | Clicks a decorative example, expects the screenshot to be an editor, or cannot locate a starting action.                |

A participant passes only when **all three** are correct without hints or follow-up coaching. Success target: **at least 4 of 5 participants pass all three**, not four correct answers per question across different people. Log setup misunderstandings separately; they may warrant copy changes even if the threshold passes.

## Layout choices

- Preserve the original animation, layout, branding and navigation.
- Explain in the existing hero paragraph that a written request becomes an animated video preview, then a selected scene can be changed and the video exported.
- Name the separately required AI assistant in the existing hero note.
- Add one example before the animated walkthrough, including the scene/time-range fallback when individual objects cannot be selected. The illustration remains an explanation, not a recording of an authenticated generation task.

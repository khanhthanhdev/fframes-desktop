FFmpeg in the fframes Studio managed SDK: license and source

Windows (x86_64-pc-windows-msvc)
  The SDK's ffmpeg folder holds the libavcodec, libavformat, libavutil, libswscale and
  libswresample DLLs of an unmodified BtbN FFmpeg build:

    ffmpeg-n9.0.2-22-g46d8f462ee-win64-gpl-shared-9.0
    https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/releases/tag/autobuild-2026-10-07-13-07

  It is configured with --enable-gpl --enable-version3 and links GPL libraries such as
  x264 and x265, so these DLLs are licensed under the GNU General Public License,
  version 3; its text is ffmpeg/LICENSE.txt in the SDK.

  Corresponding source:
    FFmpeg at n9.0.2-22-g46d8f462ee:
      https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/commit/46d8f462ee
    The build scripts, which pin the source of every bundled library:
      https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/tree/autobuild-2026-10-07-13-07

macOS and Linux
  FFmpeg is compiled from the FFmpeg source by the ffmpeg-sys-fframes crate of fframes
  (https://github.com/khanhthanhdev/fframes-desktop), with the codecs listed in the SDK's
  compatibility.json. Builds that enable x264 or x265 are licensed under the GPL.

To receive the complete corresponding source on a physical medium for three years from
the date you received the SDK, open an issue at
https://github.com/khanhthanhdev/fframes-desktop/issues.
